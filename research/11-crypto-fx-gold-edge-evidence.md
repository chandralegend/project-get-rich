## 1. TL;DR (blunt)

1. **The only crypto "edges" with repeated support are slow and boring:** time-series trend on BTC/ETH and a handful of liquid coins, volatility forecasting (for sizing, not direction), and a ranked trend factor across many coins. Even these are mostly evidenced by working papers and preprints, with 2021 data driving much of the gross return.
2. **Cross-sectional momentum in crypto is weak or size-dependent.** Time-series momentum is better supported than cross-sectional. Daily-frequency "momentum portfolios" often lose significance once real costs and liquidation risk are modelled (Han, Kang, Ryu) [snippet].
3. **Short-horizon ML on crypto prices does not clear costs reliably.** Gross positive, net fragile: a 2026 walk-forward study of BTC-USDT hourly found sign-based strategies lose profitability at 10 bps cost, and only a cost-aware trade filter rescued some configs; no statistically significant model winner [seen]. Zero-shot time-series foundation models show no convincing crypto edge [seen / snippet, thin].
4. **Funding/basis carry has largely compressed.** [own-calc] BTCUSDT perpetual funding summed to ~30.6%/yr in 2021, ~12.0% in 2024, ~5.1% in 2025 and ~2.9% in 2026 so far (Binance public data), before fees, basis risk, margin and the exchange-counterparty risk. That is at or below cash yields. The academic paper that reported high Sharpe (He, Manela, Ross, von Wachter) itself says deviations shrink over time [seen].
5. **Crypto structure risk dominates strategy alpha for a small account.** 10 Oct 2025: more than $19B liquidated, 1.6M accounts, collateral tokens (USDe, WBETH, BNSOL) briefly traded at large discounts on Binance only, and Binance paid ~$283M compensation [seen-secondary]. March 2020, May 2022, March 2023 (USDC) and 2026 gold/silver/BTC joint selloffs all show correlations going to 1 in stress.
6. **Retail outcome base rates are bad everywhere you plan to trade.** EU/UK-regulated CFD brokers disclose 51-81% of accounts lose money (ESMA 2018: 74-89%; 2026 compilation: average ~71%). Brazilian futures day traders: 97% of persistent traders lost money in one version of the paper. BIS modelled ~73-81% of retail Bitcoin app users as likely underwater. The one public Hyperliquid sample I saw: 166 of 1,000 traders profitable (unclear sampling).
7. **Gold and silver are no longer a calm diversifier.** Gold token PAXG (Binance) fell ~30% from its 28 Jan 2026 peak to today [own-calc]; silver had a record one-day crash in Feb 2026 after repeated CME margin hikes [seen-secondary, low-quality sources]. Gold trend rules that were great in 2024-Jan 2026 lost money after the peak in a long-only test [own-calc].
8. **LLM news agents:** plausible value is in *slow, semantic* tasks (classifying listings/hacks/regulatory/central-bank text into structured features and risk flags). Evidence on speed says the price moves before you can act (DeFi hacks: ~36% of the 24-hour drop occurs before public announcement [seen]). Do not build latency-sensitive news trading. Alpha Arena-type live LLM trading on crypto perps produced mostly losses [seen-secondary].
9. **Best honest plan for the profitability-index model:** make the cheap baseline a vol-scaled, multi-lookback trend/flat rule and require the triple-barrier classifier to beat it out-of-sample, net of costs, on a forward test. If it does not, the baseline is the product.

|---|---|---|

## 4. Retail outcome synthesis

- Every disclosed base rate for retail leveraged products is 51-97% losing, depending on population, product and period. None of them is *your* strategy's expected result, but they set the prior: a new, leveraged, discretionary-or-lightly-tested retail system has about a 1-in-4 or worse chance of profit.
- The mechanism that shows up repeatedly: costs, leverage-triggered ruin and no measurable learning (Brazil study), not lack of intelligence.
- There is **no honest public ledger** for retail crypto bots that I could find. Alpha Arena is the only fully public live record of agentic crypto trading and its rankings reversed within three weeks and were mostly losses. The Freqtrade docs themselves caution that hyperopt results are not reproducible.
- Implication: the system must treat its *own* ledger (paper -> micro-live) as the only credible evidence, and preregister kill criteria.

## 6. Risk-engine implications (cross-asset)

1. Two clusters, not seven positions: cap net exposure to (a) crypto-beta (BTC+ETH+alts) and (b) USD/real-rate (gold, silver, EURUSD, USDJPY).
2. Stress correlation floor: set cross-cluster correlation to at least 0.5-0.6 in stress scenarios (own-calc: BTC-PAXG correlation rose to 0.42 daily in 2026; March 2020 and Aug 2024 saw crypto, equities and gold fall together).
3. Gap and venue risk: model a -15% to -30% one-day BTC shock and a -10% one-day silver shock; maintain margin in plain stablecoins and keep spare margin on MT5.
4. Leverage: ESMA's 2:1 crypto and 20:1 gold caps (R1) are regulator judgements of what retail can survive; even outside the EU, treat them as informative ceilings for research.
5. Funding/swap as a P&L line in the ledger, and costs charged at the *worst plausible* tier in backtests.
