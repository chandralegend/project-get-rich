Get-Rich Architecture

A local-first research and trading system for crypto, gold and FX. Claude reads the news. A model you train turns market data, forecasts and that research into one number, the profitability index. A separate risk gate and you decide what gets traded.

The whole system on one page

Data flows down. Text and market data take separate paths and meet in the PI-Net. Everything above the teal line can only propose a trade. Everything on the right records what happened.

Who can touch a broker

Web text can carry instructions aimed at an LLM, and a 2026 survey found a single fake headline crashed simulated markets in every run. So the design assumes anything that reads the web can be manipulated, and gives it no way to place an order.

The profitability index model

You asked for a model that encodes forecasts and the research text together and improves as data arrives. This is that model. Five channels are encoded separately, fused, and turned into two outputs. The second output, expected net return in units of risk (R), is the profitability index.

Why the Claude channel starts off

Every Claude model's training data runs to June 2026, so its judgement on earlier dates is contaminated by memory. Only insights recorded from now on are clean. At daily bars that is about 3.6k labelled events a year across 10 assets (my arithmetic). Suggested switch-on point: 1,000 resolved events.

What it must beat

Volatility-scaled trend, HAR volatility sizing, and LightGBM on the same inputs. Research found trees often beat deep nets on this kind of data, and found no controlled evidence that text embeddings add profit. A channel that shows no lift in testing is masked off automatically.

The take-or-skip rule

Take a trade only if p is above p* = (1 + c) / (1 + b), where b is the target-to-stop ratio and c is round-trip cost in R. With b = 2 and c = 0.08, p* is 0.36. The model has to clear that after real costs, not before.

How the model keeps improving

Every decision is stored with its inputs. When the price resolves it, the outcome is written back as a label. Retraining happens on a schedule or when drift shows, and a new model only replaces the old one after it wins in shadow.

One trade, traced end to end

Pick a scenario and step through it. Each step shows the event the system writes to its log. All numbers are made up to show the mechanics, not market data or advice.

    Build order and gates
    
  
    Each phase has an exit gate. Timing is my rough guess at 10+ hours a week. Gate numbers are suggestions to tune.
    
  
    
    
    0 · Foundations
    Repo, store, collectors, the news and insight recorder, event log, status screen, external heartbeat, Mac hardening.
    Gate: recorder runs a week unattended and killing a process raises an alert.
    
    
    1 · Evaluation harness
    Cost model, purged cross-validation, Deflated Sharpe, trend and HAR baselines. Derive the drawdown limits.
    Gate: rejects shuffled labels. You approve the limits.
    
    
    2 · PI-Net, market and forecast
    Triple-barrier labels, outcome labeler, LightGBM, then the small fusion net.
    Gate: beats baselines net of 2× costs.
    
    
    2b · Raw-text channel
    Backfill GDELT and RSS, frozen embeddings, ablation.
    Gate: shows lift, or stays masked.
    
    
    3 · Agents in shadow
    Briefings and flags, then the journal, then shadow signals. Switch on the Claude channel at the event threshold.
    Gate: lift on forward data.
    
    
    4 · Risk gate + Binance testnet
    Risk gate, Broker interface, approvals, kill switch, fault injection, alert rules.
    Gate: faults raise the right alert, no stray orders.
    
    
    5 · cTrader demo
    cTrader broker on a demo account. MT5 gateway only if cTrader does not work out.
    Gate: demo fills reconcile, costs measured.
    
    
    6–7 · Micro-live
    Crypto first, then gold and FX under your own legal decision. Every order approved by you.
    Gate: live results track paper after real costs.
    
  
    

    



    
  
    What is not settled
    
  
      
    
    • Legal and funding. A 2019 Central Bank notice says residents may not trade forex or send money abroad for it. Crypto is a grey area. A remittance cap expires around 20 Dec 2026. This comes from the research agents' reading of the notices, not mine. You said you will handle it.
      • 
    
    • Binance. Futures eligibility, production API access for Sri Lankan accounts and the API terms are unconfirmed. The bulk data files forbid live trading use, so production data comes from the API.
      • 
    
    • cTrader. Which broker, whether it enables the Open API for you, real spreads and funding routes. The official Python client was last updated in August 2024.
      • 
    
    • Small account. At $400 a 0.5% risk budget is $2. A minimum gold lot likely risks far more, so the gate will skip most gold trades.
      • 
    
    • Evidence. No controlled evidence yet that text embeddings add profit, and no post-2022 confirmation for most crypto papers.
